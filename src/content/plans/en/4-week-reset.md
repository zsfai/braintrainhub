## Week 1 — Awareness and Stop-Loss

- Methods: [Environment Design](/en/methods/digital-environment/), [Daily Rhythm](/en/methods/daily-rhythm/)
- Tools: focus timer, reaction time
- Principle: [Reward Loop](/en/principles/reward-loop-short-video/)
- Output: personal temptation map (time/place/mood)

## Week 2 — Alerting and Scanning

- Tools: reaction time, Schulte (daily)
- Principle: [Attention Systems](/en/principles/attention-systems/)
- Method: offline paper Schulte twice

## Week 3 — Inhibition and Working Memory

- Tools: Stroop, N-Back
- Principles: [Working Memory](/en/principles/working-memory-basics/), [Cognitive Load](/en/principles/cognitive-load/)
- Manage load: accuracy before speed

## Week 4 — Transfer and Solidification

- Bind each training block to a real task
- Deep read: [Neuroplasticity Primer](/en/principles/neuroplasticity-basics/), [Sleep Rhythm](/en/principles/sleep-rhythm-performance/)
- Write a “maintenance list”: 2 tools + 3 anchors + 1 screen-off rule

## Whole-Program Principles

Short and regular; training is not punishment; no medical claims. If low mood or loss of control persists, seek qualified professional support.
