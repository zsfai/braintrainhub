## Week 1: Rhythm and Environment

- Fixed wake and screen-off
- Complete [Digital Minimalism](/en/methods/digital-minimalism/)
- Daily: focus session + 3 Schulte rounds
- Read: [Sleep Rhythm](/en/principles/sleep-rhythm-performance/), [Attention Systems](/en/principles/attention-systems/)

## Week 2: Control and Updating

- Daily: 1 Stroop set + 1 N-Back set + focus session
- Log error types (false starts, mind-wandering, overload)
- Read: [Working Memory](/en/principles/working-memory-basics/), [Cognitive Load](/en/principles/cognitive-load/)

## Week 3: Transfer to Real Tasks

- Right after training, 25–45 minutes real deep work (reading, writing, learning)
- Use [Attention Anchors](/en/methods/attention-anchors/) to bridge both sides
- Weekend review: which tool pairs best with deep work? Keep two main tools

## Note

If you miss more than 2 consecutive days, restart at week’s day 1 load—do not compensate with doubled training.
