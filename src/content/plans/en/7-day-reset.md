## Who It Fits

You feel short video steals large chunks of time, delays sleep, and makes daytime startup hard—but you do not need a complex long plan yet.

## Daily Fixed Actions

- Screen-off anchor (60 minutes before bed)
- One [focus session](/en/tools/focus-timer/) (start at 15 minutes)
- 10 minutes tool training: alternate [Schulte](/en/tools/schulte/) and [reaction time](/en/tools/reaction/)

## Schedule

**Days 1–2 — Stop the bleeding**  
Run [Digital Environment Design](/en/methods/digital-environment/). Log peak scroll times.

**Days 3–5 — Minimum training**  
Morning focus session; afternoon short training. Bedtime [offline drills](/en/methods/offline-drills/).

**Days 6–7 — Solidify**  
Write 3 anchors to keep for the next two weeks. Read [Reward Loop](/en/principles/reward-loop-short-video/) for deeper context.

## Success Criteria

Not “never scroll,” but: screen-off anchor hit at least 5 days; ≥5 focus sessions completed; you can name your peak temptation windows.
