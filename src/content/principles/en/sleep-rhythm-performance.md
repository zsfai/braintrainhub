Daytime “slow reactions, irritability, restlessness” is sometimes blamed on willpower alone. Research and daily life both point to **sleep duration and rhythm stability** strongly shaping available bandwidth for alerting and self-control.

## How Short Video Squeezes the Sleep Window

Pre-bed scrolling delivers strong stimulation and mood swings, pushing sleep later; bright screens and exciting content weaken “time to sleep” cues. Next-day alerting wobbles; [reaction-time practice](/en/tools/reaction/) may show higher scatter—a signal to watch, not a diagnosis.

## Practical Order for Rhythm Reset

1. **Fixed wake time** (weekends stay close).
2. **Screen-off anchor**: use [focus session timing](/en/tools/focus-timer/) for a final non-screen block before bed.
3. **Train in alert windows**: morning or early afternoon for executive-control tasks; lower difficulty when tired.

## Important Scope

This site discusses performance and habits only. It does **not** offer insomnia treatment, sleep-disorder intervention, or medical plans. For persistent severe sleep problems, seek professional help.
