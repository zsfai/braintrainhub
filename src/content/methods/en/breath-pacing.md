Impulsive scrolling happens in one or two unconscious seconds. Breath practice **inserts a pause** so executive control can engage.

## Two Basic Versions

1. **Four-count reset**: inhale–exhale for four rounds, then decide whether to pick up the phone.
2. **Inhale 4, exhale 6**: slightly longer exhale to shift from arousal toward calm (subjective experience varies).

Do 60 seconds before a [focus session](/en/tools/focus-timer/) as part of the ritual.

## Scope

This article does not address disease, panic intervention, or clinical breath therapy. If breath practice causes discomfort, stop and seek professional advice.
