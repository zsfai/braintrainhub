Focus rarely loses to “not knowing how”; it loses to **distraction within reach**. Environment design makes distraction cost one more step and real work one fewer.

## Three Layers of Change

1. **Home screen**: work and training entry only; short video on last page folder or another device.
2. **Notifications**: turn off nonessential badges; batch messaging at fixed check times.
3. **Physical**: charger outside bedroom; couch for paper books or tablet “focus mode” only.

## Linking to Training

After environment changes, run the first 25-minute [focus session timer](/en/tools/focus-timer/) daily as a new startup ritual. Theory: [Reward Loop Primer](/en/principles/reward-loop-short-video/).

## Checklist

- Does unlocking the phone show short video immediately?
- Does the browser default to news/recommendation feeds?
- Do work files take many taps to reach?

Aim for no / no / one tap—you are halfway there.
