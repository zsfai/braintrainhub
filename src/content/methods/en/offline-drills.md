Online tools log scores easily, but screens can still trigger scrolling. Offline drills offer **physical cutoff**.

## Three Easy Drills

1. **Paper Schulte**: hand-draw a 5×5 number grid, time with a watch; mirror online [Schulte tool](/en/tools/schulte/) strategy (central fixation, ordered scan).
2. **15-minute walk without headphones**: notice steps and breath—not meditation perfection, just no new information feed.
3. **Environment scan**: pick a room, find 10 red objects in order—orienting without algorithmic recommendations.

## When to Use

Bedtime, right after a scroll binge, or midday collapse wanting to “hide a bit longer.” Use offline drills as buffer, then choose deep work.
