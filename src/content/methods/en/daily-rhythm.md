Fragmented scrolling destroys not just time but **sense of time**. Anchor methods straighten the day with a handful of fixed events.

## Four Suggested Anchors

| Anchor | Example |
|---|---|
| Wake | Fixed time; avoid snooze loops |
| First deep work | Start one [focus session](/en/tools/focus-timer/) within 90 minutes of waking |
| Training window | 10-minute Schulte or reaction practice in the afternoon |
| Screen-off | 60-minute offline buffer before bed |
