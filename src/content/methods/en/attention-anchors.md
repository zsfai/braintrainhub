The expensive part of deep work is often **the first 2 minutes**. Short video is built to steal those two minutes.

## 2-Minute Ritual Template

1. Phone face-down or out of sight.
2. Open [focus session timer](/en/tools/focus-timer/).
3. On paper, write one line: “This block is only for ___.”
4. Optional: one short [Stroop](/en/tools/stroop/) warmup to activate inhibitory control.

## When the Ritual Breaks

Interruptions are normal. Do not punish with “the day is ruined”; restart from the next anchor. Plan-level forgiveness: [7-Day Reset](/en/plans/7-day-reset/).
