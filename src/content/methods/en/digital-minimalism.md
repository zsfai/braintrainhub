Minimalism targets **bandwidth**, not austerity. You want less autoplay and more intentional use.

## Steps

1. List top five apps by time in the past 7 days.
2. Mark “tool” vs “feed” types.
3. Keep one feed type; set daily cap or home-screen isolation.
4. Pre-fill freed time: walk, paper reading, three [Schulte](/en/tools/schulte/) rounds, or a short focus session.

## Intent Review Sentence

Before opening, ask: **What am I opening this to finish, and how will I exit when done?** If you cannot answer, do not open yet.

Works best with [7-Day Reset](/en/plans/7-day-reset/): minimalism creates space; the plan creates rhythm.
